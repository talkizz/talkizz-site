FAQ

What does High Quality equipment mean?
Top-end equipment, in professional terms, means HQ equipment. First and foremost for sound, from the microphones and recording gear - namely the Prism Sound audio interface and Shure - to the cables from Ghost, Klotz and Neutrik. It continues with the video and lighting equipment from Sony and Panasonic, including soft lighting that illuminates the studio with maximum evenness.
Where is the studio located and is there parking?
TALKIZZ Studio is located in Shoham, in a central and accessible spot just 5 minutes from Highway 6 and Highway 443. The location is ideal for visitors from anywhere in central Israel, Modi'in, Petah Tikva, the Sharon and the Shfela. Free private parking is available right next to the studio.
What does the podcast studio rental service include?
The studio rental includes professional top-end (HQ) equipment for sound and video, tailored studio lighting, full technical support and top-quality files ready for editing or distribution.
I don't know how to edit, can you edit my podcast?
You can receive edited files through live editing by a technician who stays with you, or basic automatic editing by speaker. In addition, you can get further editing service for an additional fee.
Can I edit it myself?
Absolutely! You can receive all the source files on shoot day directly to your hard drive / USB stick, or via a Google Drive link, and edit independently.
Can a Zoom call be connected to the podcast for a remote conversation?
Yes, you can connect Zoom or Google Meet and even broadcast them as part of the podcast.
How many studio hours do I need to record a one-hour episode?
A one-hour episode, give or take, requires 2 studio hours. Adjusting the lighting, arranging the studio as requested, the look, camera angles to taste, a sound check, closing the files and transferring them - these are some of the processes that happen when recording any podcast episode. So if you record a net hour, you should allow for another studio hour. That said, for an episode planned to run up to half an hour, one studio hour is enough.
Can I record several episodes in one day?
Absolutely! It's even recommended! It saves a lot of costs, first of all for you!
How and when do I receive the files?
Via a direct Google Drive link. If you prefer, you can bring a USB stick with you. The files will be according to your advance request for source files, automatically edited files or manually edited files. The files reach you within 24-48 hours of the shoot day (processes can be sped up if urgent!).
